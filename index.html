About us

Who we are

Skylab Rocketry is a group of amateur rocketry enthusiasts. We build model rockets, launch them, and share the results with anyone who loves rockets and space.

Our story

In june 2023 we founded the Space Propulsion Laboratory (SPL) as a way of learning how rocketry and phisics work by working closely with it. In november we had our first successful launch, the DragonFlame V, leading to much more vehicles and flights in the upcoming years. In may 2026 we decided to change our name and branding to Skylab Rocketry, and today we're still chasing the same objectives of 2023: science, communication and teamwork!

Our approach

Learning by building

We learn by designing, building and flying our own rockets, one launch at a time.

Safe, responsible launches

We follow safe launch practices and always fly responsibly, with the right recovery systems and procedures.

Sharing what we learn with the community

We document our builds and launches so that anyone curious about model rocketry can follow along and learn with us.
